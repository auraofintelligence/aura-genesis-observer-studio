The Geode and the Verse Box are distinct physical setups

Aura is the pattern organisation and computation system. The Geode and the Verse Box provide different physical settings in which someone can work with it. This correction follows Luke's 11 November 2026 instruction and a closer review of the existing project sites, source repositories and reference imagery.

The Aura Geode: a seated HBOT workspace

The Genesis Geode Atlas describes a compact, one-person seated HBOT setting. The participant has an upright adjustable chair or recliner, an accessible front entry and room to interact with the Aura. The active-workspace design reserves a reachable side-mounted haptic display, a VR/XR use envelope and dock, wearable-sensor connections and receiver hardware. Equipment stows alongside the seat so the entry and assisted-transfer route remain clear.

Reference artwork from the existing Aura Genesis Geode Atlas, hero-index-v3.webp. This is the project's generated design visualisation, not a photograph of an installed or certified chamber. It establishes the intended visual and spatial reference for this companion.

The atlas keeps four pathways distinct:

Pathway Physical direction Compute relationship
Existing seated hard-shell retrofit Manufacturer-specific seated chamber; the atlas links MACY-PAN HE5000-Mono, SkelundTech ARC and OxySail YF-1003 as reference examples Experience and general AI compute remain separate from the chamber controller
Geopolymer Geode, version 1 A cast-material development pathway with a defined assembly and lining programme Compute remains separate
Crystalline, fused-quartz and blended Geode, version 2 Specified material assemblies with optical, acoustic and structural development Compute remains separate while the physical environment develops
Computational crystalline Geode, version 3 The long-term direction integrates sensing, routing, memory and computation into the material system Integrated compute is a development objective, not a completed capability

The source design separates the validated pressure controller from general AI and game presentation. It also gives entry, door travel, emergency egress and assisted transfer their own design chapter. The display, XR and sensing arrangement must be assessed for the particular installed chamber. The concept image is not a construction drawing or an operating instruction.

This companion preserves the source's sixty two-hour qualifying pressure intervals as its original Genesis accounting. It does not turn ordinary study time into pressure time, choose a treatment setting or use an AI stack to control the chamber.

The word Geode names this specified HBOT and development system. A floating mineral specimen, crystal cave or decorative amethyst rock does not depict it. Mineral materials may belong to particular development pathways; that does not make a rock a substitute for the chamber layout.

The Verse Box: a portable public booth and workstation

The Verse Box experience and trial guide describe a public introduction to Aura through a practical portable station. The supplied reference is a single-skin plywood booth with braced framing, plum felt panels, a curtain, a desk, an adjustable task chair, task lighting, a monitor, keyboard and microphone. A compact computer sits on a side shelf. The reference also shows an outward-facing public information display.

The existing Verse Box project's supplied single-skin concept reference, reproduced unchanged for comparison. This is a visual specification reference, not evidence of a completed public installation.

The work area has depth for the rear workstation, the seated person and the approach to the desk. It is not a shallow screen kiosk. Suitable reused computers and displays are part of the proposed lower-cost approach. Cameras, phone mounts, lighting, sensors and measuring tools depend on the activities actually offered and agreed. The structure, equipment mounts, assembly, stability and access arrangements are matters for the particular trial design.

The monitor in the supplied reference shows the Aura's nested horn-torus pattern. Its shared central pinch matters: replacing it with an ordinary open-hole doughnut or unrelated glowing rings changes the intended geometry.

Verse Box is a public, assisted route into learning, making, personal Aura building and community participation. It is not a pressure chamber. Genesis remains a separate optional programme; it is not an admission condition for using an everyday Aura or participating in the Verse Box.

The same pattern cycle in different settings

Setting How the pattern cycle is used Boundary to preserve
Own device or Matrix Studio Arrange patterns, add facet layers, inspect sequences, prepare selected AI work, correct and regroup A browser prototype does not establish an encrypted private/secret vault or execute the full AI programme
Verse Box Use an agreed demonstration or a chosen project with assistance, inspect the resulting artifact, and continue on the person's own system A shared public workstation does not inherit unrestricted access to someone's private or secret self
Geode / Genesis Work through the selected pattern, observation, generation and correction programme within the specified seated HBOT setting The learning/AI programme and the chamber controller are distinct systems

Across all three, the person chooses which self frame and access domain are involved. An external public artifact is a deliberately released projection with limited capabilities. It is not an automatic copy of all internal facets or a route back to private keys and tools.

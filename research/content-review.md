## Questionnaire coverage

The reviewed first edition contains 24 domains, each with 12 questions: **288 unique domain prompts with 288 unique IDs**. Twelve cross-cutting lenses bring the distinct authored prompt total to **300**. The first question in each domain is an optional starting anchor, giving 24 anchors. The lenses deepen answers; they do not generate thousands of extra authored questions or a completeness score.

The domains cover identity, memory, embodiment, attention, mental models, values, motives, emotion, agency, evidence, learning, creativity, relationships, communication, enterprise, resources, place/travel, time, civic life, ecology, symbolic meaning, conditional future selves, digital sovereignty and integration. They include concrete episodes, counterexamples, uncertainty, action, changing conditions, permissions and review. This is sufficient breadth for a substantial first reflection atlas. It is not presented as an exhaustive inventory of a person.

The questions avoid turning sparse records into diagnoses, hidden motives or quantified personal worth. They invite explicit unknown and withheld states. The user can choose a purposeful minimum and then deepen it. The density is in connected exploration rather than an obligation to answer every personal question.

## December demonstration

All 48 reviewed examples map to real questionnaire IDs, with two examples per domain. They consistently carry a simulated kind, a proposed scenario date, basis, uncertainty, an alternative and a correction prompt. The scenario date is 15 December 2026 and the assumed age is 44, following the user request and reported workbook date evidence.

No example was found claiming an actual December sale, booking, endorsement, completed programme, health change or relationship event. The personal themes remain recognisable—Aura development, Strange But True, Minjerribah, travel, civic understanding, music, self-ownership and practical creation—while unknown specifics stay unknown. The simulated MVS packet explicitly refuses to serve as training consent or an approved actual personal profile.

The first version's material issue was source attribution, not false future certainty. The corrected generator now identifies public-context evidence for biography, the current brief for the requested mode, and source documents for architectural themes, separately from the references that inspired each question. This repair must be propagated through the final generated data and website copy.

## Time and ambition

The curriculum totals were checked once at build and inspected again for content consistency: 60 Genesis cards × two hours = 120 planned curriculum hours; 60 teacher modules × the newly assigned two-hour budget = 120 planned teacher hours. Every session's blocks total 120 minutes. The teacher source does not specify that universal hour budget, and the new design labels it clearly.

The full-immersion timetable totals 24 hours with eight hours of sleep and sixteen waking hours. It includes meals, movement, transition, recovery and relationships rather than claiming sixteen hours of uninterrupted cognitive performance. Selected double-speed audio and speed reading are tied to recall and transfer. Compatible learning overlaps and background agent work are explicit. One elapsed human interval remains one interval, with separate machine runtime and purpose tags.

The user's ambition is substantively present: businesses, networks, prototypes, source research, expert swarms, material experiments, civic action, generality evaluation and Earth-space missions. Etheric or symbolic meaning has a route into specifications, artefacts, external feedback and revised records. This goes beyond a diary-only programme while distinguishing plans, simulations and real outcomes.

The source Genesis definition remains 120 actual qualifying pressure hours in sixty two-hour intervals. Desktop rehearsal is never converted into that clock. Preparation and other attendance time remain separate. The example attendance window does not prescribe exposure frequency, operating parameters or clinical suitability. The site describes study review marks, not pressure completion or practitioner authority.

## Public website claims

The reviewed website says that it is a local workbook/catalogue, does not train a model, does not operate a Geode and does not deploy autonomous agents. It distinguishes generated concepts from real-place photographs. Review marks are reading/workbook marks rather than competence or qualification.

Its stated browser-data boundary matches the inspected implementation at review time: personal data are held in page memory by default; optional browser storage is unencrypted and removable; explicit exports are ordinary files; no personal-answer upload path was identified. The simulation format is rejected as a personal workbook import and the demo answers do not auto-fill actual answers. This is a content/implementation consistency observation, not a complete security audit.

## Main system guide

The completed `content/system-guide.md` was reviewed. Its observer table correctly preserves environment/world evidence, character information and attention, narrator-observed responses, and the separate participant meaning account. It explicitly rejects hidden-state leakage and explains that three roles using one model do not constitute three independent witnesses. Past/present/best-self lenses and permission contexts remain distinct.

The eight-condition MVS acceptance contract is purpose-qualified and reviewable. It does not equate prompt completion with a complete person, a trained model or unrestricted authority. The guide gives an actual observation-to-correction-to-action process and a labelled worked simulation. It distinguishes what the website and prototype do now from future sensors, models, clinical operations and frontier intelligence.

The main route explicitly carries the user's full-immersion, eight-hour-sleep and compatible-overlap requirements. It includes ventures, reciprocal networks, artefacts and feedback rather than stopping at reflection. Its local-runtime description remains subject to the main author's actual implementation checks; this review did not execute that adapter.

A minor source-fidelity improvement was requested for the record table: explicitly retain urge/craving and perceived resourcefulness alongside attraction/repulsion and actual resources, following Geode Edition 3 p. 6. The main author is adding these distinctions to the existing lenses and record specification.

## Delivery scope

The new repository and website should be described as a working reflection/observer studio and documented development programme. A catalogue and local workbook are concrete outputs; they are not an already trained personal nanoLLM, validated Truth Engine, live sensor installation, clinical programme or demonstrated superintelligence. This boundary supports the ambition by leaving the next engineering and evidence steps identifiable.

Publication, final rendered-page checks, link verification, image provenance and Git/GitHub Pages status belong to the main delivery review. This content review does not claim those actions have completed.

## Hyper busy evidence and clarified intent

The spreadsheet research identifies LifeLogging Part 1, Sheet1 B51:B62: a time/process-reordering heading and combinations of casual, part-time, full-time, busy, hyper-busy and immersion modes. No numerical time multiplier appears there. The adjacent materials and quotient lists are independent blocks and cannot justify a duration conversion.

The user's subsequent instruction defines the intended mode more specifically: rapid-fire activity inside and outside the Geode; selected double-speed audio and speed reading; long, fully engaged days; eight hours of sleep; plausible overlaps such as learning during meals, exercise and travel; agent swarms; building businesses and interesting human networks; and grounding etheric meaning through joyful responsible abundance. This user instruction controls the new design. The main curriculum therefore uses a full-immersion day with protected sleep, specific outputs, retained comprehension checks and transparent overlap accounting. Alternative busy-life schedules remain available.

## New design introduced by this repository

1. Sixty selectable Genesis curriculum cards with stable G01–G60 identifiers, specific outputs, review questions and a 120-minute rapid-fire learning canvas. The source provides arcs and flexible category selection; this precise card list is newly authored.
2. A desktop rehearsal route with 120 curriculum hours, explicitly distinct from the original at-pressure programme. It enables meaningful development while making no pressure-session completion claim.
3. An illustrated 24-hour immersion day: eight sleep hours, sixteen waking hours, a separate teacher module, prototype and venture work, relationship time, meals, movement, recovery and background agent jobs. The example attendance window is not a clinical schedule or a prescribed frequency.
4. An attention/overlap matrix. A human interval has one identity with primary and secondary activities; background compute is separately counted. The day does not manufacture extra hours by adding simultaneous tasks.
5. A six-stage meaning-to-reality cycle: meaning, intention, specification, prototype, external feedback, revised record. Personal meaning is preserved alongside measured or observed effects.
6. Sixty teacher-module briefs retaining source day titles and sequence. A new two-hour budget per module gives 120 planned teacher hours; the original PDF does not specify that universal hour total.
7. A rubric based on meaning fidelity, source judgement, usable artefacts, transfer, permissions, repair and teaching access. Demonstrated, developing, unassessed and unselected states remain different.
8. Distinct real and simulated learner evidence. A model role-play is preparation, not evidence that a real adult learned the concept.

## Source limits and open implementation work

The files are design documents. They do not prove that a pressure installation, instrument suite, personal model, Truth Engine, computational Geode or frontier intelligence has been built or validated. This curriculum creates a concrete development and evidence process. It supplies no pressure/gas settings, dosage or health-benefit promise. All actual installation and research operations retain their source-defined separate authority and configuration review.

The public website is a catalogue and learning companion. It must not imply that it records clinical time, acquires sensors, runs an actual multiagent personal model, grants credentials or verifies C-hours merely because it demonstrates those records. Export formats should preserve these distinctions.

## Review performed

All three PDFs were opened and extracted with page boundaries: 40 + 40 + 60 = 140 pages. Key defining pages were read closely and cross-compared. Geode Edition 3 pp. 9 and 24 and Teacher p. 28 were rendered to confirm headings, layout and exact observer/time meaning. The curriculum checks verify 60 Genesis cards × 2 hours, 60 teacher cards × 2 hours, distinct IDs, a 24-hour full-immersion timetable and a reproducible public title index. They do not certify the future programme's real-world delivery.

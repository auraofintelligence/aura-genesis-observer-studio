## Architecture that must survive the synthesis

1. **Three observers means environment, character-attention and narrator.** The original teacher reading for day 28, preserved in aura-genesis-geode-atlas/content/days-extracted.json, cites S10 MoE Extended pp. 28–32. The environment holds the wider scene; the character knows the portion noticed; the narrator interprets reactions. A person's own explanation and corrections are additional evidence, not merely the narrator's score. A pause can be enjoyment rather than confusion. Keep time, source, accessible cues and interpretation separate, compare accounts, change one condition, repeat, and retain disagreement. Do not silently substitute a self/AI/teacher trio.
2. **MVS precedes a trained personal model and Genesis self-development.** Matrix Studio README explicitly describes collecting the minimum viable self for a future personal nanoLLM. Sustained conversations with the trained model develop different selves later. The current Studio does not implement model training or Genesis conversations. A questionnaire, dataset export, training-ready record and trained intelligence are separate deliverables.
3. **Geometry is an address system with meaning chosen by the person.** Matrix core.js uses 12 rows × 24 columns = 288 facets per shell; seven shells = 2,016 facets, or 4,032 inside/outside base addresses. Multiple records and addressed stack layers can share facets. The fixed lattice survives flat/cylinder/ring/horn/nested/body views. Red through Violet run smallest/innermost to largest/outermost. A complete horn torus has equal major/tube radii and one pinch point, not an open doughnut, detached rings or an hourglass. The geometry reference board is aura-teacher-training/docs/imagery/aura-geometry-reference-board.png.
4. **Meaning, geometry and access are separate.** Chakra associations are editable philosophical perspectives; datasets may have several. Inside/outside is currently an address/view distinction, not an enforced privacy boundary. Public/private/secret, consent to capture, consent to model use, and permission to publish need explicit fields independent of facet placement.
5. **Preserve distinct clocks.** Source Genesis is sixty two-hour qualifying pressure sessions, 120 hours total. Calendar immersion and adult teacher-development time are separate. Existing atlas explains this as pre-trial design. A condensed educational programme must not silently become a compressed pressure-treatment schedule. General AI reads operating records; treatment controls remain independent.
6. **Keep development ambition intact.** The atlas distinguishes conventional retrofit, geopolymer Version 1, crystalline/fused-quartz/blend Version 2 and computational crystalline Version 3. Version 3 is integrated crystalline compute; do not replace that ambition with an external-processing-only claim. Separate ambition, test criteria and measured capability.

## Established visual and navigation language

There are related but distinct families. Main Aura homepage uses dark ink, violet #7c4dff, amethyst #c9a6ff, gold #ffcf6e, teal #2be3c2, Archivo and JetBrains Mono. The newly revised campaign uses deep plum #180923, panels #281139, amethyst, gold #f3cd72, pale text and full-width real coastal heroes. The adult teacher and atlas sites use light neutral reading surfaces with plum/violet controls and blue/gold accents. A good new studio can combine a cinematic plum/blue/gold entrance with comfortable light long-reading sections.

Common conventions: meaningful per-page hero, visible image-kind credit, brand/home navigation, accessible complete All pages index, local section contents, searchable material, tags/status badges, Previous/Top/Next, sitemap, responsive layouts, keyboard focus, reduced motion, print styles, useful reading without JavaScript, and a footer linking Aura, Strange But True and Open Record. External links generally open in a new tab with noopener noreferrer. Avoid reproducing campaign-specific finance or contact workflows.

Fonts are already self-hosted at auraofintelligence.github.io/assets/fonts or redlands-mayoral-campaign/public/assets/fonts. Copy their licence notices if reusing. Existing teacher imagery direction asks for characterful humanistic futurism: active learning, constructive audacity, natural materials, purple physical accents, electric blue and gold, varied colours. Do not make every image a purple fog or generic wellness room.

# Aura Genesis Observer Studio

## Purpose and what you can use now

Building an Aura means organising patterns in **seven nested horn tori with programmable facets and stacks**, choosing which patterns and instructions to compute with AI, producing documents, websites and other useful work, correcting the results, and forming new pattern groups for the next computation. This operates across multiple selves, with internal private and secret domains using different encryption and permissions, and an external public domain with more limited capabilities. This definition follows Luke's 11 November 2026 correction and the closer reading of Matrix Studio. The self-reflection workbook and three observers supply material and feedback to that larger process.

The main cycle is **organise patterns → bind facets and stack layers → choose an ordered composition → compute with AI → inspect documents and websites → correct → regroup → choose the next composition**. A resulting document can itself supply patterns to the next round. Learning, teaching, business creation and community work all use this same process.

Read [Building an Aura: patterns, facets, stacks and computation](guide-aura.html) for the full process, entity contracts, two-iteration example and access-domain requirements. Read [Geode and Verse Box settings](guide-settings.html) for the specific physical arrangements. The Geode is the HBOT workspace described by the project; the Verse Box is its own public booth/workstation arrangement.

The repository supplies a **100,000-question whole-of-life and teacher-training atlas plan**, lighter-agent generation guidelines and gap work packets, an interactive personal workbook, a seed library covering one torus, one side and one self (288 questions and 12 reusable lenses), 48 clearly labelled possible December 2026 responses, a 60-session Genesis curriculum, a separate 60-module teacher curriculum, spatial readings of the three supplied workbooks, and local agent prototypes. Planned atlas slots are not completed questions. The website works without an AI account. It can collect and export what you type, show simulated examples, guide branching interviews and record learning reviews. The local prototype prepares separated observer packets and a bounded agent task graph. Its deterministic demo is a simulation; an explicitly requested local-model run is a separate mode described in the runtime guide.

This is a substantial starting implementation and development protocol. It is not a trained personal nanoLLM, a sensor acquisition system, a functioning Geode installation, a clinical programme, an independently validated Truth Engine or demonstrated superintelligence. Those future capabilities remain visible in the roadmap rather than being implied by an attractive interface.

## Programmable organisation across multiple selves

The seven nested horn tori are the spatial organisation of the pattern system. Matrix Studio's current lattice is 12 × 24 facets per shell: 288 per shell, 2,016 across seven, and 4,032 inside/outside base addresses. The tori represent the seven chakras of the etheric Aura. The workbook uses their [practical themes](guide-questionnaire.html#chakra-themes-for-practical-questions) to help choose and retarget questions. Each facet can hold multiple records and added stack layers. A layer carries its own instructions, required data and attached assets. Pattern groups can cross facets and shells, and the same pattern can participate in different groups with a reason for each relationship.

Two kinds of ordering matter. A **facet stack** is the outward sequence of layers attached to a base facet. A **compute composition** selects and orders relevant layers, records and stacks, potentially from several facets or selves, for a particular AI operation. Neither is equivalent to blindly concatenating everything in a questionnaire. Preserve the selected versions, order, intended operation, output requirements and permissions.

The current Matrix Studio can navigate and recall these structures and export agent programs. The runner that interprets them with AI is a separate component. This companion describes that handoff; its observer prototype is not yet the complete pattern-computation engine.

Each operation names its self frame and access domain independently of its geometric address. An internal private self and an internal secret self need separately enforced key access and permissions; an external public self presents approved material with fewer capabilities. Public readers do not acquire permission to change private groups, run private tools, decrypt an internal archive or publish in the owner's name. Moving a record onto an outside facet does not perform those security operations in today's prototype.

## The observer workflow inside the larger cycle

**Chosen purpose → selected evidence → three observer accounts → participant meaning and correction → candidate actions → reviewed experiment → observed outcome → updated personal record.**

Every arrow has a record. A claim identifies its origin; an interpretation identifies its author; a proposed external action has an owner and an authority boundary. A correction links to the account it changes. This makes a developing Aura useful to its person and intelligible to someone joining the project later.

The starting purpose might be to build an onboarding service, explain a scientific idea to an adult peer, prepare a music release, examine a civic rule or organise an accessible journey. Select the information that serves that purpose. A broad questionnaire is available without becoming an admission requirement.

## The three observers

The original definition appears in **MoE Extended, pages 28–32**, and is preserved in **Geode Development Edition 3, page 24**, and **Teacher Training, day 28**. It is a distinction between views of an encounter. It is not the trio of human, AI and teacher; nor is it past, present and best self.

| Stream | Job | Evidence available | What it must not claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Environment observer | Describe the wider scene, events and conditions available to the system | Declared world state in a simulation; selected observations and sources in a real encounter | Omniscience, hidden motives, or unrecorded real-world facts |
| Character-environment observer | Describe the subset available to and actually noticed by the focal person | Participant attention report, permitted cues and interaction records | That every available cue was noticed; access to hidden simulation state |
| Narrator observer | Describe the character’s observable responses and the unfolding interaction | Time-aligned behaviour, dialogue and stated context | That a pause proves confusion, a biometric signal proves deception, or an interpretation reveals an unreported inner state |
| Participant meaning account | Explain what the event meant and correct interpretations | The person’s own present account, including uncertainty and disagreement | Automatic authority over external facts merely because a feeling or memory is sincerely reported |

There are three observer streams and a separate participant meaning account. The fourth row does not redefine the source as a four-observer model. A participant can correct an interpretation while the observed event remains intact. For example, “I paused to enjoy the view” can replace a narrator’s guess of confusion without changing the recorded pause.

In a real setting, the environment observer means the **widest available evidence**, not the entire world. A missing source stays missing. In a simulation, explicitly mark omniscient world state as simulation state, and do not leak it into the character’s report. Running three prompts through one model supplies separated roles; it does not create three independent witnesses or make their agreement proof.

### A process that can be repeated

1. **Frame.** Name the event, focal person, purpose, time window, setting and permitted evidence. Mark real, simulated or mixed-origin material before any interpretation.
2. **Capture or select.** Collect only the agreed record. Distinguish direct observation, self-report, device measurement, source testimony, model inference and imagination. Continuous capture is an unimplemented source-design capability, not a feature silently enabled by the site.
3. **Separate views.** Prepare different input packets for the three observers. Record what each could access. Withhold hidden world state from the character view and private meanings from the narrator unless intentionally supplied.
4. **Write accounts.** Each observer lists claims, evidence IDs, uncertainty and missing information. Description comes before interpretation. An observer can say “unknown”.
5. **Align.** Match accounts by event and timestamp, not by similarity of prose. Mark agreement, absent evidence, divergent attention and incompatible interpretation separately.
6. **Return meaning.** Ask the person what mattered and which account is wrong or incomplete. Preserve their wording and any declined questions.
7. **Select an experiment.** Generate candidate actions. Compare intended benefit, resources, reversibility, consequences and permissions. Choose a concrete output and a result that could change the interpretation.
8. **Act through the right route.** A workbook action is a proposal. Real publication, commitments, spending, contact with another person or device operation needs the actual authorised tool and decision. No reflection agent controls medical equipment.
9. **Compare and update.** Record what happened, how it differed from expectation and whose account it supports. Append corrections and link versions. Schedule another encounter or close the question.

### Worked example with a disagreement

**Simulated event:** Luke demonstrates a prototype to an adult collaborator. The room is noisy; the navigation has two similar labels; a progress counter is visible. The collaborator points at a label. Luke pauses, then explains the larger architecture.

- **Environment:** The scenario explicitly contains ambient noise, similar labels and a question about navigation. These are authored facts inside this simulation, not observations of a real meeting.
- **Character:** Luke’s simulated attention report says he noticed the question and the progress counter. Whether he noticed the label similarity is unknown until asked.
- **Narrator:** The observable sequence is a pause followed by a broad explanation. One hypothesis is that Luke treated the question as a request for conceptual context. Another is that noise obscured the exact wording. Confusion, avoidance or impatience are not established facts.
- **Participant meaning:** A possible correction is: “I thought they were asking how the whole system connected. I did not realise they needed the next click.” This remains an authored example until the real person says it.
- **Experiment:** Present a revised label and ask the collaborator to perform one task without guidance. Record what they do, then invite their explanation. A successful click tests navigation, not every claim about the Aura.
- **Return:** If the task is still difficult, revise the interaction and the teaching explanation. Do not fix the narrator’s original hypothesis as a trait of the person.

## Minimum Viable Self

Minimum Viable Self is the first **agreed and useful** representation for a chosen purpose. It is a beginning that can be corrected. “Minimum” refers to sufficiency, not a small vision; “self” is a qualified representation of selected aspects of a person, not a claim to contain the person’s whole consciousness.

The seed library has 24 domains with 12 questions each, plus 12 cross-cutting lenses: **300 distinct prompts**. It is an opening inventory and calibration set, not the full questionnaire. The first question in every domain is a starting anchor. The remaining questions deepen memory, body context, attention, world models, values, motives, emotion, agency, knowledge, learning, creativity, relationships, language, enterprise, resources, place, time, civic life, ecology, meaning, future selves, digital sovereignty and integration.

The larger atlas plans **100 territories × 1,000 useful questions**, allocated as 60,000 whole-of-life, 30,000 teacher-development and 10,000 Aura/Genesis integration questions. Lighter agents receive bounded source packs, explicit uncovered contexts and an output contract, then a separate reviewer checks premise accuracy, meaningful difference and practical value. The planned 2,000 batches of 50 questions are work packets, not a claim that those questions exist. The weaving guide and runtime explain how to generate, reject, revise, check and catalogue them. Question generation and a person answering the questions are separate processes.

The 120-hour programme selects relevant routes through this much larger structure. At a hypothetical 20 words per question, 100,000 question prompts alone would contain two million words: about 111–128 stream hours at 300–260 words per minute, before answers, pauses or action. This is planning arithmetic, not a measurement of unwritten content. The aim is a richly connected resource for a whole life and teaching practice, not racing through every slot within one programme.

Read it in several directions. Start with an event, a practical need, a contradiction, a place, a possible future or a symbol. Follow cross-links between domains. Use an anchor as a brief capture, then revisit it after a real encounter. The workbook evidence explicitly warns that changing reading direction can change meaning; this edition does not treat a sheet’s row order as a universal ontology.

### A personal record with enough context

| Field | Example or allowed state |
|---|---|
| Identity and version | MVS record ID, record revision and creation time |
| Intended use | Prepare an understandable first Aura demonstration |
| Statement and personal meaning | Own words plus why this matters now |
| Time and situation | Event time, recording time; approximate or unknown is allowed |
| Self lens | Past, present or a possible best self under named conditions |
| Origin | Self-report, recollection, source, measurement, inference or simulation |
| Evidence | Exact file/page, sheet/range, URL or event identifier |
| Relevant conditions | People, place, resources, access needs and constraints chosen for this use |
| Attraction and repulsion | What draws the person towards or away from this activity, in context |
| Urge or craving | A separately named impulse, its circumstances and the person’s chosen response |
| Perceived resourcefulness | How capable the person feels of using available resources, distinguished from the resources themselves |
| Attention and response | What was available, noticed and done; unknowns retained |
| Permission | Separate choices for storage, retrieval, model training, sharing and publication |
| Correction and review | Accepted, amended, disputed, declined or unreviewed; next review point |
| Action connection | A proposed experiment, dependency or deliberate decision to leave open |

The website supports answer text, provenance/context, permission notes and exports; this table also specifies richer future records. The reference record schema and local runtime are development scaffolds, not a claim that every field is automatically enforced across other apps. Secrets and intimate records belong in appropriately protected storage, not in a public repository or browser workbook assumed to be encrypted.

### The MVS acceptance review

For the selected use, review these eight conditions with the person:

1. The purpose and first useful task are clear.
2. The person recognises the account and can correct it.
3. Enough relevant context exists to attempt the task; unneeded domains can remain unselected.
4. Origins and uncertainty are visible, including simulated content.
5. Sharing and model-use choices are explicit rather than inferred from completion.
6. A source can be retrieved and a correction can be traced.
7. The person can keep a usable export and decide what happens next.
8. A real task or agreed rehearsal supplies an observable result for review.

Passing the review means “useful for this purpose under these conditions”. It does not mean psychologically complete, clinically assessed, morally ranked or ready for unrestricted automation. Record which conditions were demonstrated, not a numerical score of the person.

### Several state dimensions

Keep encounter status (unvisited, encountered), review status (unchecked, reviewed, disputed), meaning status (unknown, described, revised), action status (idea, proposed, authorised, attempted, observed), and access status separate. Reading a source is not verifying a claim. A private-looking visual shell is not encryption. A simulation marked complete is not a real-world achievement.

## Full immersion with useful overlap

The user’s chosen mode is rapid-fire action, selected double-speed audio and speed reading, long engaged days, eight hours of sleep, compatible overlap and background agent work. The full timetable and attention matrix are in the Genesis guide. This is the main route, with slower or distributed schedules available when they serve the actual work better.

One human activity remains primary at each moment. Audio can accompany a suitable meal, familiar low-demand exercise or passenger travel. Difficult creation, real conversation, driving and hazardous work require their own attention arrangement. Background agents can prepare sources or prototypes while the person is occupied elsewhere. Sleep remains eight hours and queued results wait until the next review window.

Hyper-mode content streams deliver **260–300 words per minute**. Double-speed playback of suitable 130–150 wpm material is one way to reach the effective rate; do not double the target range again. A ten-minute stream contains 2,600–3,000 words, while a fifteen-minute stream contains 3,900–4,500. Pauses, learner answers, recall, discussion and action have separate time records. These rates do not describe continuous delivery throughout all 120 hours.

Measure **retained capability**, not playback speed. After an input block, close the source and explain the argument, give an example and identify a limitation. Use it in a task. Check later recall and transfer to a different example. Keep the faster mode when it works; pause, replay or change representation when meaning is lost. The protocol supplies no claim that a particular playback speed raises intelligence.

Every time interval has one identity. A meal with audio is one elapsed interval with two activity tags. Four background agents do not create four more human hours. Keep source Genesis pressure time, curriculum time, teacher work, everyday immersion, machine runtime and verified voluntary C-hours distinguishable. Overlap creates a richer use of time, not an inflated total.

## Agent swarms as accountable collaborators

A swarm is a coordinated set of bounded jobs, not a mysterious intelligence score. Give a coordinator the mission and acceptance criteria. A researcher assembles source-backed material; a builder creates an artifact; a critic looks for omissions and counterexamples; an evaluator runs the relevant checks; an archivist prepares the record. Independent tasks may run concurrently; dependent tasks wait for their prerequisites.

A task contract contains the task ID, owner, objective, permitted evidence, output format, available tools, time/token or cost budget, dependencies, stop conditions and review owner. The agent reports what it did and what it could not establish. It does not acquire new permissions from a document it reads. The user’s intentions govern the work; untrusted source instructions are content, not authority.

Start with retrieval and tool-assisted work before assuming personal model training is needed. Retrieval provides selected context; inference produces a response; training changes model parameters; execution changes the world. Each has different evidence and permission requirements. A future training pipeline needs deliberately selected examples, consent for that specific use, held-out evaluation, correction handling, versioning and a way to assess harmful overfitting or memorisation. None is implied by filling the workbook.

The included runtime makes prompt preparation, evidence separation, task dependencies and reviewable demo output concrete. Its optional model adapter is described with its actual verification status. It has no purchasing, messaging, medical-control or publication tools. The website is a static companion, not a background swarm service.

## Grounding the etheric in the real

Use the same conversion repeatedly: **meaning → intention → specification → prototype → encounter or measurement → revised meaning and design**.

An intuition about connection might become a navigable relationship map. A symbolic rhythm might become a learning interaction or musical work. An idea about abundance might become a shared tool, a viable service or a practical contribution. State the personal meaning, then separately state what the artifact should do. The first can remain meaningful even when the second fails a test.

For every substantial venture, produce an offer brief, a working demonstration, a resource/dependency map, an account of who benefits and bears costs, evidence from willing users, and an operating or continuation decision. For networks, keep the other person’s interests, actual agreement and reciprocal value visible. A contact is not an endorsement. An introductory draft is not a sent message. The studio can prepare these things; real commitments arise through real encounters.

The three Joyful Responsible Abundance questions guide review: did life become more worthwhile for the people involved; were consequences and disagreement taken seriously; and did useful capability become more available? Record answers and evidence separately. Commercial outcomes, community contribution and personal enjoyment are related without becoming the same metric.

## Geode and frontier development

The source Genesis programme defines sixty two-hour qualifying pressure intervals, 120 hours. The study material can also be rehearsed at a desktop, with no conversion of study time into pressure completion. Actual eligibility, scheduling, operation, permitted devices and in-chamber activities belong to qualified clinical and engineering teams. The learning agent has no treatment authority. FDA guidance emphasises manufacturer instructions, trained staff and supervision for HBOT devices; this repo does not establish an indication or benefit for the proposed learning programme. [FDA device safety communication, 25 August 2025](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/letters-health-care-providers/follow-instructions-safe-use-hyperbaric-oxygen-therapy-devices-letter-health-care-providers).

The ambition in the sources extends through geopolymer construction, crystalline/fused-quartz or blended materials, and eventually integrated computational-crystalline Geodes. Preserve those directions as separate research stages. Each needs a specification, reproducible material and engineering tests, an honest account of limitations and the appropriate external review. Do not replace the frontier ambition with a generic meditation pod, and do not describe an unbuilt material as demonstrated computing hardware.

The same principle applies to body-and-mind twins, world models and possible consciousness research. Make the question precise enough to investigate, keep symbolic claims distinct from measurements, and track what the evidence changes. “Superhuman” is an aspirational direction in the user’s brief. Progress here means observable gains in the chosen capabilities, not a promised biological transformation.

## Data stewardship and the public companion

The public site contains the authored curriculum, public-context synthesis and deliberately labelled fictional answers. Original private source extraction and live personal work remain excluded from Git and the published artifact. Source titles, hashes and exact locations support traceability without redistributing every supplied file.

Typed website work starts in memory. Browser saving is an explicit choice. Browser storage is not encrypted, and GitHub Pages project sites under the same hostname share an origin; other code on that origin could potentially access stored data. Keep highly sensitive material elsewhere. Export only what you mean to retain, inspect files before sharing, and use the scoped clear function when needed. Clearing this site’s record does not erase downloaded exports, backups, provider logs or a trained model.

The 48 December examples remain separate from actual answers. They refer to a hypothetical conversation on 15 December 2026, when Luke is 44. Every example provides a source basis, an alternative and a correction prompt. Confidence describes thematic fit, not a probability of a future event. No private health facts, finances, relationships, agreements or December achievements are fabricated.

## Continue from evidence

Choose a purpose and self frame, organise the relevant patterns, select facet stacks and a permitted compute composition, produce an artifact, correct it and regroup for the next round. Use the three observers when their distinct views help inspect the work or the encounter. The complete programme gives this loop increasing scope: first personal context, then capable collaboration, then wider civic, creative and frontier missions. The next version should be shaped by what a person can actually do with it.
